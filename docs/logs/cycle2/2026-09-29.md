## P11 (29 Sep)

### Trust incident: `/themes/[theme]` showed deal badges on prices older than 12 h (P11 item 6)
**What:**
- `/themes/[theme]` lists sets with prices and deal badges (`set_price_summary`, PR-B #228, live 26 Sep 16:29 UTC).
- It had **no revalidate**, so its 29 pages were built once per deploy and served unchanged until the next one.
- A badge only needs an in-stock row ≤ 12 h old **at build time**, so every hour a page outlived its build data's 12 h mark was an hour of badges shown on prices older than 12 h (G14).

**How long** (each build's data = the last scrape completed before its build job; a deploy's build runs before its approval wait):

| Build (go-live, UTC) | Data from scrape | Badges pass 12 h | Replaced | Stale-badge time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 26 Sep 16:32 (16:54) | 26 Sep 15:57 | 27 Sep 03:57 | 27 Sep 02:21 | 0 |
| 27 Sep 02:17 / 02:52 | 26 Sep 20:45 | 27 Sep 08:45 | 27 Sep 16:34 | **7 h 49 m** |
| 27 Sep 16:30 (16:34) | 27 Sep 11:34 (the 16:33 scrape ended after the build) | 27 Sep 23:34 | 28 Sep 16:18 | **16 h 44 m** |
| 28 Sep 16:14 (16:18) | 28 Sep 13:09 | 29 Sep 01:09 | 29 Sep 01:42 | **34 m** |

- **Total:** about **25 h** between 27 and 29 Sep. The longest single stretch was **16 h 44 m** (27 Sep 23:34 → 28 Sep 16:18 UTC).
- **Not quantified:** how many visitors saw it. We have no per-theme-page view data for that window.

**Closed by #427** (live 29 Sep 01:42 UTC; the audit verified `/themes/[theme]` at 6 h):
- A theme page now regenerates every 6 h, so the "until the next deploy" exposure is gone.
- **Residual, by design and the same as priced set pages:** a badge rendered from a row just under 12 h old can stay up to 6 h, plus the time until the next request triggers regeneration.
- **Guard:** the #427 CI audit fails any route whose effective revalidate drifts from its registered cadence.
