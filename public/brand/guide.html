BRAND GUIDELINES · V1.0 · JULY 2026

Bricks of India

Every Brick Tells a Story

The visual identity system for bricksofindia.com — India's LEGO® price comparison, reviews and community site. Use this guide for the website, YouTube channel, social posts and merchandise.

01

Logo & Wordmark

Two marks, one hierarchy. The stacked BRICKS OF INDIA wordmark is the primary logo. The minifig icon is the secondary mark for avatars, favicons and small spaces.

PRIMARY — STACKED WORDMARK
BRICKS
OF INDIA
"BRICKS" always in Brick Yellow #FFC72C, "OF INDIA" always in white, both with the black keyline. Never recolor, italicise or set on one line.
SECONDARY — MINIFIG ICON
For favicons, app icons, YouTube avatar and watermarks. Minimum size 24 px. Keep the full figure visible — never crop below the shoulders.
Clear space
Keep space equal to the height of the letter "B" clear on all sides of the wordmark.
Minimum size
Wordmark: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print. Below that, use the minifig icon.
Backgrounds
Best on Sky Blue, Navy or photography. The black keyline keeps it legible everywhere.

03

Typography

Two Google Fonts, already wired into the site. Fredoka is round and toy-like for headlines and buttons; Inter does the reading and the rupees.

HEADINGS · FREDOKA
Every Brick Tells a Story

Weights 500–700. Used for h1–h3, buttons and badges. Sentence case for headlines; ALL CAPS only for small labels.
BODY & PRICES · INTER

04

The Mascots

Two minifigs, both named Abhinav (it's printed on their backs). Red Abhinav wears the red jersey, cap and orange goggles; Blue Abhinav wears the blue hoodie with the heart. They are the face of every review, deal and video.

Red Abhinav
The Critic
Cap, goggles, red jersey with the gold star. Fronts reviews, verdicts, rankings and "is it worth it?" content. Owns the trophy.
Blue Abhinav
The Enthusiast
Blue hoodie with the red heart. Fronts welcomes, deals, community and celebration moments. Owns the phone.
POSE LIBRARY — WHICH POSE, WHEN
Red · Thumbs up
Positive verdicts, recommended sets
Red · Thumbs down
Skip-it verdicts, overpriced sets
Red · Judging
Review thumbnails, ratings
Red · Reading
Guides, long-form articles
Red · Pointing
Callouts, 'look at this' moments
Red · Trophy
Best-of lists, award picks
Blue · Welcome
Hero sections, onboarding, about
Blue · Celebrate
Deal alerts, milestones
DO
  • Use one mascot per composition; both only for hero/about moments
  • Match the pose to the emotion of the content
  • Place on white, Sky Blue or photographic backgrounds
  • Keep them large — they are characters, not icons
DON'T
  • Don't mirror, recolor or redraw the figures
  • Don't crop heads or rotate more than ±5°
  • Don't use a negative pose (thumbs-down, shocked) next to a partner brand
  • Don't add speech bubbles longer than one short line
05

Voice, Tone & Taglines

Warm, witty, straight-talking. We're fans first — but fans who check the price three times.

PRIMARY TAGLINE
Every Brick Tells a Story
The brand line. Use under the logo, in the site hero, video intros and press material.
SECONDARY LINE — THE WINK
Where Everything Is Awesome, Except Financial Advice
The personality line. For YouTube banner rotations, t-shirts, stickers, website section headers, end cards and merchandise — never as the main lockup.
We say
"₹18,999 at three stores. Wait for Diwali." · "This set is gorgeous. Your wallet will disagree."
We never say
"BUY NOW!!!" · "Limited time only!" · corporate jargon, fake urgency, or anything we can't verify.
House style
Indian English, ₹ with Indian grouping, LEGO® on first mention, honest verdicts even for sponsored sets.
